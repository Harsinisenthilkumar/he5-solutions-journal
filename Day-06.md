## Topics Covered

---

## Concepts Learned

### Real-World Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering becomes more effective when prompts are designed around specific business requirements and expected outcomes.

### Context Enhancement

Providing sufficient context helps improve response relevance and accuracy.

### Role-Based Prompting

Assigning a specific role enables the model to generate more focused responses.

### Structured Outputs

Clearly defining output formats improves consistency and usability.

---

## Activities Performed

- Experimented with prompts across multiple use cases.
- Applied role-based prompting techniques.
- Tested structured output formats.
- Analyzed response quality and consistency.
- Identified best practices for prompt design.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Context significantly improves output quality.
- Structured prompts provide more predictable responses.
- Role-based prompting improves task-specific performance.
- Prompt reusability reduces development effort.
